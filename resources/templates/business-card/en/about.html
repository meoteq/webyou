About me

I like making complicated things simple.

I am a designer from a small town. I started with drawings and posters, and now I help people build websites that make sense at first glance.

How I work

  1. First we talk about who the website is for.
  2. Then I draw a simple layout and show it to you.
  3. After the revisions I build the pages and test them on a phone.

Things I enjoy

  • Warm colors and large text.
  • Clear buttons without extra words.
  • Websites that open fast.
